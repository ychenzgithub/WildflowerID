Bloom calendar花期日历

Darker cells mean more of the flower's iNaturalist records were made in bloom that month: California records for the California and garden groups, and the mountain national parks for the Canadian Rockies. Garden plants with few wild records use their typical garden season. Rows are sorted by peak month; the current month is highlighted.颜色越深，表示该月 iNaturalist 记录中开花的比例越高：加州野花和园艺花卉使用加州的记录，落基山野花使用山区国家公园的记录。野外记录很少的园艺植物使用其在花园中的典型花期。按盛花月份排序，当前月份高亮显示。
